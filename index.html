            DAY 1 ASSIGNMENT — 10 INTERACTIVE ACCORDIONS
            
How to Validate Polygon Topologies and Standardize Coordinate Reference Systems for Subnational Boundary Analysis in GeoPandas

          


          

            

              
1. EPSG:4326 vs. EPSG:32646

              
EPSG:4326 uses angular decimal degrees. For Bangladesh ($88^\circ\text{E}$ to $92.7^\circ\text{E}$), reproject to UTM Zone 46N (EPSG:32646) (or EPSG:32645 west of $90^\circ\text{E}$) before computing $\text{km}^2$.

            

            

              
2. Ring Self-Intersections

              
Digitized union/upazila shapefiles frequently contain self-intersecting rings that cause gpd.sjoin or gpd.overlay to fail with TopologyException.

            

            

              
3. .make_valid() vs. .buffer(0)

              
While .buffer(0) can silently invert interior holes, modern GeoPandas (≥1.0 with Shapely 2.0) provides .make_valid(), repairing rings safely.
